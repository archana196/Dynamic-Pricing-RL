# Evaluation Metrics

The following metrics were used to evaluate pricing strategies.

## Revenue

Total revenue generated during an episode.

Higher value indicates better pricing performance.

---

## Reward

Cumulative reinforcement learning reward.

Measures how effectively the agent balances pricing and bookings.

---

## Bookings

Average successful bookings.

Higher bookings indicate better inventory utilization.

---

## Comparison Criteria

- Maximum Revenue
- Highest Reward
- Highest Booking Rate
- Pricing Stability
