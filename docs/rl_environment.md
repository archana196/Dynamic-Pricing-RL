# Reinforcement Learning Environment

## Environment

DynamicPricingEnv

## Observation Space

Inventory Ratio

Remaining Days Ratio

Current Price Ratio

Competitor Price Ratio

Booking Probability

Demand Level

---

## Action Space

0 → Decrease Price

1 → Keep Price

2 → Increase Price

---

## Episode Termination

Episode terminates when

- Inventory becomes zero

OR

- Booking window expires.
